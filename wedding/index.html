Our story

A love story, with a little chocolate

We met over coffee and stayed for dessert. Years of Sunday brunches, long drives through the Durbanville hills and far too many shared slices of chocolate cake later, we're making it official.

We've chosen a small, cosy bistro for a small, cosy wedding: a handful of the people we love most, good food, good wine from the valley and a fireplace to gather around. We would love for you to be there.

The venue

Bistro Chocolat Durbanville

Chocolat Bistro is a charming spot in Durbanville, about 30 minutes from central Cape Town. We will celebrate in the Birdcage, its private room with its own bar and fireplace, and spill out into the garden for drinks and photos.

Good to know

Dress code

Garden-party chic in our colour scheme: black, charcoal, grey and eucalyptus greens. Block heels are kind to the garden lawn.

Weather

January in Durbanville means long, hot summer days and warm evenings, often with a southeaster breeze. Dress for the heat, bring sunglasses for the garden and a light layer for later.

An intimate guest list

The Birdcage seats a small party, so we can only host the guests named on your invitation. Thank you for understanding.

Food & dietary needs

Dinner is served by the bistro kitchen. Tell us about allergies or dietary needs in your RSVP and we'll make sure you're looked after.

Staying over

Durbanville has guesthouses and wine-farm stays a few minutes from the bistro. Book early, as January is peak holiday season in the Cape.

Gifts

Your company is the gift. If you'd like to give something, a contribution to our honeymoon would be warmly received.
