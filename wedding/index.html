Notre histoire

A love story, with a little chocolate

We met over coffee and stayed for dessert. Years of Sunday brunches, long drives through the Durbanville hills and far too many shared slices of chocolate cake later, we're making it official.

We've chosen a small, cosy bistro for a small, cosy wedding: a handful of the people we love most, French food, good wine from the valley and a fireplace to gather around. We would love for you to be there.

A French bistro in the heart of the wine valley

Chocolat Bistro is a little piece of Paris in Durbanville, about 30 minutes from central Cape Town. We will celebrate in the Birdcage, its private room with its own bar and fireplace, and spill out into the garden for drinks and photos.

Bon à savoir

Good to know

La tenue

Dress code

Garden-party chic. Think linen, florals and colours of the chocolatier: cocoa, caramel, blush and cream. Block heels are kind to the garden lawn.

La météo

Weather

January in Durbanville means long, hot summer days and warm evenings, often with a southeaster breeze. Dress for the heat, bring sunglasses for the garden and a light layer for later.

Les invités

An intimate guest list

The Birdcage seats a small party, so we can only host the guests named on your invitation. Thank you for understanding.

À table

Food & dietary needs

Dinner is a French-inspired menu from the bistro kitchen. Tell us about allergies or dietary needs in your RSVP and we'll make sure you're looked after.

Où dormir

Staying over

Durbanville has guesthouses and wine-farm stays a few minutes from the bistro. Book early, as January is peak holiday season in the Cape.
